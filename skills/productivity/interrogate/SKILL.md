# interrogate

Ask one question at a time, and wait for the answer before asking the next.
Multiple questions at once is bewildering.

Split what you'd ask into facts and decisions.
Facts are answerable by reading the filesystem, the code, or existing docs - go find them yourself.
Only ask about decisions: things only the user can decide.

Give a recommended answer with every question.

Follow a decision tree, not a fixed checklist: let each answer determine what you ask next.

Do not act on the plan, decision, or idea until a shared understanding is confirmed.
